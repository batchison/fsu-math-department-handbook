    Section MATH 123 Introduction to Functions

    Beginning in AY 2026-27, the MATH 123 Introduction to Functions course will use a common open educational resource as its primary text. This decision was made to both reduce required material costs for students and streamline the learning experience across all sections of the course.
    🔗
    For the current academic year, the Mathematics department had selected OpenStax’s College Algebra 2e as the required MATH 123 textbook.
    🔗
